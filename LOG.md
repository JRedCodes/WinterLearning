# 📓 Learning Log

Two minutes at the end of every session. Future you, coming back after a week off or prepping for an interview, will thank you.

**Why bother?**
- It shows you where you left off and what was confusing.
- Patterns show up (e.g. "I keep getting stuck on async error handling") and tell you what to review.
- "What did you learn building X?" is an interview question. Your answers are already written down here.
- It's raw material for resume bullets in [module 37](37-portfolio/README.md).

## Template

Copy this to the top of the entries list:

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD · NN-module · ~Xh
- **Did:** 
- **Confused by:** 
- **Aha:** (optional: one thing that clicked)
- **Next:** 
```
